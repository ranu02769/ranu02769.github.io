Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Overview

Subsy ("we", "our", or "app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines how your data is handled when you use the Subsy Android application.

2. Data Collection & Storage

Subsy is an offline-first application. All subscription details, costs, billing dates, notes, and custom categories you enter are stored strictly on your local device using SQLite. We do not transmit, sync, or sell your personal financial records to any external servers.

3. Advertising

To support free development, Subsy displays non-intrusive advertisements served by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID) to serve relevant ads per Google's Privacy & Terms. Users can upgrade to Subsy Pro for a 100% ad-free experience.

4. Notifications

The app requests notification permissions strictly to trigger local scheduled alarms for upcoming subscription renewals and trial end dates. No data leaves your device.
