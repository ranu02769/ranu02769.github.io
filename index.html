Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

1. Commitment to Privacy

Subsy is developed with a strict offline-first philosophy. We believe your personal finances should remain solely in your own hands. We do not operate databases that store your personal subscription costs or billing cycles.

2. Data Storage & Architecture

All subscription records, renewal dates, payment frequencies, and custom notes created by you are saved locally on your Android device using SQLite/Drift storage. Subsy does not upload your entries to remote servers.

3. AdMob & Third-Party Advertising

The free version of Subsy displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID) and non-precise location to display advertisements in accordance with Google's Advertising Privacy Policies. You can upgrade to Subsy Pro for a permanent, 100% ad-free experience.

4. Device Permissions

POST_NOTIFICATIONS / SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Used solely to trigger local renewal reminders and trial expiration alerts you have chosen to set.

USE_BIOMETRIC: Used locally to lock/unlock the app using your device's fingerprint or Face ID. Biometric data is handled entirely by Android's hardware security layer and cannot be read by Subsy.
